Your offline sky studio

Getting started

  1. Open Local Sky Studio in a modern web browser using the website link.
  2. Enter a date, local time, latitude, longitude and time zone. The sky redraws automatically.
  3. Set the style and export size. Click Download SVG, then open that file in Illustrator.

Your own constellation illustrations

This version does not include your animal/figure drawings because they have not been provided. A prepared line-art pack stores each stroke in J2000 sky coordinates after a one-time alignment. Once loaded, the same artwork is projected automatically with the stars for every date and location. You can hide individual illustrations and edit the exported SVG groups. Raw PNG, JPG and SVG artwork cannot be placed accurately just by uploading it; prepare and check its anchors first.

Privacy

Star calculations, coordinate handling, artwork handling and exports happen locally in your browser. No location service or analytics receives your coordinates. Exported SVG metadata contains the date/location you enter: treat those files as private.

Chart assumptions & accuracy

Designed for decorative star maps between 1900 and 2100, not navigation or professional astrometry. Stars are taken from the XHIP/Hipparcos catalogue in J2000 coordinates via D3-Celestial. Astronomy Engine supplies precession, nutation and Earth rotation for the selected date. This chart uses a geometric horizon at sea level; atmospheric refraction, terrain, annual aberration, parallax and stellar proper motion are not applied. Close to the horizon, visibility can differ from an observed sky. Large date ranges and fast-moving stars can differ slightly from a full planetarium model. Moon, Sun, planets, Milky Way shading and daylight filtering are not included. This is not a pixel-for-pixel recreation of another website.

Named time zones use your browser's timezone database. Nonexistent DST times are rejected. For an ambiguous repeated hour, select Fixed UTC offset. Check unusual historical offsets yourself. Modern time zones cannot resolve every historical local convention.

SVG uses your selected physical dimensions. PNG has the pixel dimensions needed for 300 pixels per inch at that size, but an image editor may show a different DPI metadata value; set its print size to the selected millimetres. Names remain text and may use a fallback font when moved to another computer.
